coordination decision record is not lost. The primary decision table lives in
`../DECISION_prereg_coord_v1.md` and `../summary_*.csv`.

|---|---|

| `REAL_COHORT_RUNBOOK.md` | Runbook for the real-cohort coordination check (as-run paths) |
| `highk_dataset_level.csv`, `highk_program_level.csv`, `highk_realized_k.csv` | High-K scenario outputs |
| `probe_ebcp_selection.csv` | eb_cp component-selection probe output |

These are historical as-run records with absolute sandbox paths. They are not
regenerated by any maintained script, confer no new acceptance, and the
preregistered conclusion (no arm adopted; `pa_cc` remains the default) is
unchanged. The real-cohort runbook does not authorize biological acquisition.
