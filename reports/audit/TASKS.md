## Workstream E protocol (pre-registered before execution)

Scope: bounded pilot comparison of the cellprograms ebmf pipeline against cNMF (Kotliar et al. 2019, eLife 8:e43803) on the audit's planted-truth simulator family. This is NOT the registered MR-2 adoption benchmark (which requires the full cmp-bench-v1.2 held-out and its own preregistration); results are audit evidence only.

- Data (shared generator, matched seeds across arms): donor-pseudobulk, n=80, p=600.
  - Recovery scenario: K_true=3 (blocks of 30 genes, loading effect 1.0, donor scores z ~ N(0,1) + noise sd 0.3), 10 reps (seeds 1-10); pure-noise scenario K_true=0, 10 reps (seeds 11-20).
  - Detection scenario: 2 cell types x 10 programs, planted pair A_1-B_1 at rho in {0.3, 0.45, 0.6} + null, 20 reps (seeds 1-20), site strata (2 levels).
- Arms: (1) cellprograms ebmf — fit_celltype_programs(max_factors=10, point_laplace, var_type=1, seed=rep) on log-scale pseudobulk + canonicalize_programs; detection via find_coordinated_programs(n_perm=199, strata="site", ambient="none"). (2) cNMF (Python, cNMF package) — on counts = round(exp(Y)) (its native multiplicative/count scale; deviation documented); recovery: K grid 1-8, 10 restarts, K selected by usage-stability across restarts; detection: K=10 FIXED (true K given to cNMF; ebmf gets cap=10 with automatic retention — generous-to-comparator), 15 restarts, cross-type matching by usage correlation at |r| > 0.5 (0.7 sensitivity).
- Metrics: K selected vs K_true (incl. noise); score/usage recovery (cor to planted z); top-30-gene block recovery (Jaccard); detection power (planted pair matched); false-CMP rate on null (any cross-type pair matched); seed reproducibility (ebmf bit-identical across seeds — re-verified on 2 pairs; cNMF consensus stability across seed pairs); runtime per fit.
- Known deviations (documented): cNMF restarts below its default 100 (bounded compute); donors treated as observations (cNMF is cell-level by design; donor level is the package's scope); cNMF K selection by stability argmax (its guidance allows local maxima).
